Important: This Cookie Policy explains how AlgorithmOptix IT uses essential and optional cookies or similar technologies, including analytics and marketing tools where enabled, and how you can manage your cookie preferences.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains the browser storage and third-party technologies used on algorithmoptix-it.xyz.

2. Essential storage

The site stores your cookie choice in local browser storage under algorithmoptix_cookie_consent_v1. This is necessary to remember whether you accepted or rejected non-essential tracking.

3. Marketing technology

Meta Pixel may be loaded only after you choose to allow marketing cookies. It can help measure advertising activity and may send information to Meta according to Meta's own policies. The current site does not require marketing consent for normal page access.

4. Contact-page services

Google reCAPTCHA is used on the contact page to reduce spam. Opening the external Google Maps link or interacting with third-party social links may allow those providers to process information according to their own policies.

5. Managing your choice

Use the “Cookie Settings” link in the footer to change your marketing-cookie preference. You can also clear site data in your browser. Rejecting marketing cookies does not prevent you from viewing the site or submitting a contact enquiry.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when tracking tools or site functions change. Questions can be sent to info@algorithmoptix-it.xyz.
